# Meeting follow-through

Deploy the website update **before** the Discord bot update. This extends the existing meeting JSON; there is no new SQL migration. Existing scheduled meetings, proposal and onboarding controls, interview invitations, role scopes, and the 15 automatic delivery retries remain in place.

- A positive availability reply needs at least three future 30-minute slots across two different calendar dates in the participant's selected timezone. The two-hour booking notice remains. An explicit **No suitable times** is still a valid reply; expired slots cannot turn into one. Active first-round applicants with older one-day availability are asked to add a second day; already booked meetings are untouched.
- The staff timer starts when the bot requests that staff member's availability, after the applicant replies. At 24 hours it sends a private reminder. At 48 hours it excludes that unanswered staff member from the quorum and tries to DM them and post a Staff mention. The applicant and two-thirds of the remaining staff must still match and be free; at least one attending staff member is mandatory. An explicit reply of no suitable times is not treated as silence. A late reply can rejoin matching until the booking is reserved.
- Private booking announcements include the submission's selected interests, application role or enquiry subject and confirm the booked availability. They do not include long-form answers, other availability or private staff notes.
- In **Another meeting**, Founder / CEO is selectable. The bot looks for a conflict-free slot after the original meeting day in the applicant's timezone, using the selected participants' still-valid saved times and eligible standing staff availability. If that cannot book, the normal availability requests begin again. The database still makes the final reservation under its existing concurrency lock.
- After seven days without a booking, including archived no-match/expired and paused invitations, the bot posts one Staff attention card per round/re-ask cycle. The card identifies the meeting, explains the stall, and offers **Re-ask availability**, **Add staff**, and **Open meeting settings**. Re-ask respects current notification/department access. Add staff is server-admin-only and restricted to current human members with the Staff role, excluding the applicant.

Bot alerts default to channel `1308354117625708614` in the configured guild and the unique Discord role named **Staff**. Set `WEBSITE_MEETING_STAFF_ROLE_ID` if the name is different or ambiguous. Optionally use `WEBSITE_MEETING_STAFF_CHANNEL_ID` to change the channel. The bot needs view, send, read-history and role-mention capability there. If no Staff role resolves, messages are posted without a role ping.

Outstanding meetings are picked up automatically after deploy; weekly cards are delivered in batches. Do not test by sending dummy production invitations or Staff pings.
